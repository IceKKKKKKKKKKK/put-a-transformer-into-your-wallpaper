# Demo recording

[Watch or download demo.mp4](https://github.com/IceKKKKKKKKKKK/transformer-explainer-wallpaper/raw/refs/heads/main/media/demo.mp4).

This updated user-recorded demonstration shows the installed black Transformer Explainer wallpaper with its **local GPT-2 small / CUDA backend** on the Windows 11 desktop, with the actual bottom taskbar visible throughout. It includes interaction with the prompt, attention diagram, MLP explanation, and sampling controls. The larger model/device status and simplified header are visible.

The MP4 is 64.4 seconds long at 1920 × 1080 and 30 fps. The supplied recording's duration, playback speed, framing, and audio are preserved. FFmpeg adds the original team's credit, the exact phrase below, the paper citation, and the original application URL in the unused space above the visualization. The interaction and taskbar are part of the recording; they are not composited into it. This clip predates the mouse-pointer workaround and is not evidence that the workaround resolved a particular machine's input behavior.

The GIF is a silent, smaller version of the same full clip at 1280 × 720 and 10 fps. The poster is a still from the updated video.
